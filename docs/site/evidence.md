## Evidence still required

The four current-state dossiers and candidate-neutral fixture have landed. Their unresolved access and observation gaps remain explicit evidence requirements rather than negative findings. The next bounded gate is to run the minimal custom API baseline against the frozen fixture, then produce the Alder, Northwind, and combined Acme adoption fit-gap analyses while carrying those gaps forward. Candidate spikes, decision-specific tests, and ADRs follow only when their [decision gates](decisions) are actually unlocked. The executable scenarios and category-specific interpretation remain canonical in the [synthesis report](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/3230e1326e2f2280c6d79ff6dc83fd2d60988137/issues/native-agent-host-architecture/synthesis-and-architecture-review.md#7-frozen-common-hands-on-benchmark-specification).
