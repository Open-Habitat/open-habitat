---
title: Start contributing to Open Habitat
goals:
  - title: Get to know the project
    what: You understand the main parts and have one small first task.
    steps:
      - Read the project instructions.
      - Walk through the browser, the gateway and the daemon with the agent.
      - Pick the suggested first task.
    when: When you open this repository for the first time.
    prompt: Read the project instructions and show me where the browser, the gateway and a person's own daemon meet. Suggest one small first task without changing code.
  - title: Pick up an issue
    what: One ready issue is understood and its next step is clear.
    steps:
      - Find a ready issue in the DAG.
      - Go through its goal and acceptance criteria.
      - Agree on the next step.
    when: When you want to work on an existing issue.
    prompt: Find a ready issue with me and suggest its next step.
    skill: issue
---
Open Habitat brings **native agent sessions to the browser**. The boundary
between people is their Linux account: the browser owns neither the agents'
history nor their credentials.

Read `AGENTS.md` and `TESTING.md` first. Choose a goal below or ask your own
question.
