# role-carol

Carol, designer and artist. The three glimpses the page shows (logo, tote,
cut-outs) now end in real files made by the programs her agent runs in the
story; the two reached from the sidebar (raw files, poster) are as round 6
left them.

## What changed and why

- **Tote film.** The CSS box that stood in for the bag is replaced by two
  Blender renders: one full turn of a linen tote with a block print, in
  indigo and in rust, on a paper sweep. The story now says what makes it
  worth wanting: the tote is not sewn yet, and she gets a product film of it
  before lunch is over. The player is the film with one small play button
  instead of the browser's control bar, which laid a dark gradient over the
  product. It turns on its own while it is on screen; with reduced motion it
  is a still with the button.
- **Cut-outs.** Twelve "photographs" (Blender renders of a tote, a cushion
  and a pouch on the print table, with tins, books, a brayer and pinned
  proofs behind them) were cut out by rembg and set on white with the same
  margin by ImageMagick. The view shows one before and after and a contact
  sheet of the twelve. The three marked "check" are the three rembg really
  got wrong (a piece of the ink roller kept twice, a pouch's tab lost), and
  the reply says so.
- **Logo.** A 200-pixel JPEG of a studio logo, traced by vtracer into six
  paths. Both panes carry a lens on the same spot at five times the size,
  because at the size of the view a soft JPEG and a vector look alike. The
  reply admits what the trace did badly (the two rust dots).
- **Work worth opening.** The commands under "Show work" are the ones
  `assets.mjs` runs, with their real figures (frames, samples, path count,
  file sizes). Durations were corrected to fit that work.
- Sidebar lines: "Traced: 6 paths", "Two spins ready".

## Assets and how they were made

`node blocks/role-carol/assets.mjs` writes everything to
`$ETUSIVU_ASSETS/role-carol/`; `scene.py` is the Blender scene. Stages can be
named (`masks`, `render` or its halves `turn` and `photos`, `encode`, `cut`,
`logo`), and intermediates go to `$CAROL_RENDERS`, so the render can run on
another machine than the rest.

| File | Made by |
|---|---|
| `tote-indigo.mp4`, `tote-rust.mp4` (800 × 600, 5 s, 120 frames) | Blender 4.5.16 LTS, Cycles on the CPU, 32 samples with OpenImageDenoise: `blender -b --factory-startup -P scene.py -- --mode turntable --ink <ink> --print sprig.png --frames 120 --res 800x600 --samples 32`; then ffmpeg 6.1.1, `libx264 -crf 20 -pix_fmt yuv420p -an -movflags +faststart` |
| `tote-indigo.webp`, `tote-rust.webp` | the first frame of each, ImageMagick 6.9.12 |
| `photo-01.webp` | `scene.py --mode photo --item tote --ink indigo --seed 11 --res 1000x750 --samples 48` |
| `shop-01.webp`, `cut-01.webp` … `cut-12.webp` | rembg 2.0.87 (`rembg i -m isnet-general-use`) on the twelve photo renders, then `convert -channel A -level 6%,100% +channel -trim +repage -resize 1280x1280 -background white -gravity center -extent 1600x1600 -flatten` |
| `logo-2016.webp` | the logo drawn in `assets.mjs`, rasterised to 200 px (rsvg-convert 2.58) and saved as a JPEG at quality 38; the WebP is that JPEG stored losslessly, because the budget takes no JPEG |
| `logo-2016.svg` | the JPEG enlarged 8×, blurred, remapped to the three logo colours and median-filtered (ImageMagick), then vtracer 0.6.15 (`color`, `stacked`, `spline`, speckle 100, path precision 1): 6 paths, 47 KB |

The bag, cushion and pouch are meshes generated in `scene.py` (no model
files); the linen is a procedural material; the three block-print patterns
are SVGs written by `assets.mjs` and used as ink masks.

Blender publishes no Linux arm64 build, and the round's host is arm64, so
the `render` stage ran on an x64 Linux machine (official
`blender-4.5.16-linux-x64.tar.xz`, checksum verified) and the frames were
copied back; every other stage ran on the round's host. rembg and vtracer
were installed with uv into the scratch directory.

The vtracer 0.6.15 wheel for Linux arm64 crashes when its options are passed
by keyword; `assets.mjs` passes them by position.

## Assumptions about the product

- The agent can unpack a program into the person's home and run it without
  an administrator (the Blender and rembg rows), as round 6 already assumed.
- A view can show a folder's result (`render/out`, `team/photos/winter`) as
  pictures and a film, not only a single file.

## For Jari

- The story's figures are the real ones of these files (120 frames, 800 ×
  600, 32 samples; 12 of the 104 photos exist). The 48 minutes is what the
  two turns took on eight shared CPU cores, rounded.
- The film starts by itself when it is on screen (about 0.4 MB each), which
  departs from the kit's "nothing loads until the reader presses play". It
  sells the turn; say if the page should stay still until asked.
- The logo's cream background is part of the traced file; a transparent
  version is not shown, so the reply no longer promises one.

## Engine needs

- `video()` always draws the browser's controls and never loops. A product
  film wants `loop` and no control bar; this block rewrites the attribute
  string and adds its own button (`.cr-play`, `client.js`). An option on
  `video()` (`{ loop, controls: false }`) and a shared play button would
  serve other blocks too.
- `.pg p` rules in the shared CSS outrank a block's one-class selectors for
  paragraphs in a view; this block prefixes `.scr-view-b` to win.
