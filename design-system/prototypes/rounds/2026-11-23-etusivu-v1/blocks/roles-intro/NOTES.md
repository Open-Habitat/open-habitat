- Headline "Six colleagues, each with agents of their own." This carries the through-line: one server, each person in their own account, many agents.

The claims match the role blocks: each person has their own Linux account, files and agents, and work passes through shared folders (`team/…`, `bookkeeping/…`, as every role's `hand` line shows), with history that says who did what.
- Avatars are slightly larger, there is a hover lift (off under reduced motion), and the phone shows a two-column grid instead of a ragged wrap.
