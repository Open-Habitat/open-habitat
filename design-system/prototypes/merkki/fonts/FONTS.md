# Wordmark fonts for the mark bed

Subsets (Basic Latin, ÄÅÖäåö, dashes, apostrophe) made with `pyftsubset` from
the variable fonts in [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) `ofl/`,
2.11.2026. All are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 ([OFL-1.1.txt](OFL-1.1.txt)),
and none has a Reserved Font Name, so a subset may keep its name.

embeds them unmodified from google/fonts instead.
