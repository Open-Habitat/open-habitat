# Why offered views may not embed iframes, and whether that should change

## Description

Jari 28.11.2026: offered views run under a strict policy that does not allow
another iframe inside the view. This gets in the way of some uses, especially
when the nested frame would point back to Habitat's own content (another
offered view, a file, a workspace page). Is the restriction justified, and
why is it there?

Agreed next action: research (reasons, threats, alternatives); a decision
follows from it.
