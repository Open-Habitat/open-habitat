# Prototype: settings selectors (segments, steppers, many-choice)

Jari 1.11. (pilot.572, Appearance in English): the settings selectors look odd. The *Language* segmented control wraps to two rows (System · Suomi · English (US), then English (UK) alone) and is not as wide as the *Theme* control above it. The *Recent conversations* stepper has its number at the right of a wide field and small − / + (that stepper goes away with `rail-quiet-all-flat`, but the stepper control is used elsewhere, e.g. Omat luvut in recap settings). Prototype the settings selectors as a family (segmented choice, stepper, a choice of many options such as language) before fixing: baseline as on main, 2–3 directions, desktop and phone, light and dark, Finnish and English labels (English is longer).
