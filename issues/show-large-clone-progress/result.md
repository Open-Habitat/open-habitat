Missing/malformed terminal responses remain uncertain. Existing native trees must be preserved and migrated deliberately rather than hidden by a mount. Verify the mount survives reboot and starts before the daemon socket/service; test absent mount, insufficient headroom and an exhausted volume in the disposable test guest.
