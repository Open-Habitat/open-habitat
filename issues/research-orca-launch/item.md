# Research: how Orca was marketed, and how to launch Open Habitat as open source

## Description

Jari 31.10.: Jari will publish Open Habitat as open source and wants to know how to do it. Orca (Stably AI, github.com/stablyai/orca) grew to ~111k GitHub stars in about seven months (see `issues/benchmark-orca/report.md`). Research how: a dated timeline of its launch and marketing (Show HN / Launch HN, Product Hunt, X/Twitter and who amplified it, YouTube and influencers, Reddit, Discord, newsletters, YC, release cadence and changelog as marketing, README and website, demo videos, comparisons, integrations with popular agents, star-growth inflection points and what caused each), what was organic and what was paid or network-driven, and what critics said. Then a concrete launch plan for Open Habitat: what to have ready before going public (README, demo, install path, licence, positioning against Orca and HumanLayer), which channels in which order, what to say (its real difference: many people on one server as Unix users, browser-first, native tools), and what a small team can realistically sustain. Report in Finnish with sources in `report.md`; separate what sources say from inference.
