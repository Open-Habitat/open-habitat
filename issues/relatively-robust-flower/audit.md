# Browser UX audit, round 2 — synthetic lab

**Baseline:** `d3c6e269c9c8a377561dae8e3dc12f7bd01b062c` (checked-out main-derived tree before this issue). **Recommendation:** restore identity-safe stale reading on transient failures first; then make account identity legible and reduce duplication in the workspace landing screen. Do not redesign working first-use or send flows on the basis of the previous audit.

## Defects — prioritized

### 1 · High · A transient read failure removes the conversation being read

**Where / experience.** Open a populated native history, switch the fixture to `error`, then use the rail's **Päivitä**. The entire rail and history disappear, replaced by *Yhteyttä ei saatu / Työtiloja ei voitu näyttää*. [Before](screenshots/history-markdown.png) · [after a refresh without navigation](screenshots/outage-after-read.png). `App.tsx` dispatches `unavailable()`; `web/src/app/store.ts` clears the snapshot even while the same tab still holds a session. This is the old audit's item 2, now verified as a **populated-view transition**, not just a fresh load.

**Why / fix.** The agreed [katkos rule](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/90ef1565f1df46da9ade73b885db2d5bb2c4a837/docs/ui.md#katkos-ja-epävarma-tulos) keeps last verified content readable, labelled stale, with writes disabled. Retain the already verified, identity-bound snapshot only while the same authenticated identity can be trusted; display a visible stale/retry state without destroying the reader's location. Clear immediately on expiry, logout or an unverified identity switch. Test interruption after populated history and after an active chat, plus Alice→Bob after logout. Never use stale data to enable writes. This needs security review of the identity boundary, not an unconditional cache.

### 2 · Medium · Switching accounts is hard to verify visually

**Where / experience.** Alice and Bob both show **Oma tili** in the rail and **Tili: Oma tili** in settings; their synthetic workspaces overlap heavily. After signing out Alice and signing in Bob in the *same tab*, Alice's new `first` checkout disappears (good), but the remaining screen offers no recognizable account identity. [Alice settings](screenshots/settings.png) · [Bob settings](screenshots/bob-settings.png) · [Bob workspace index](screenshots/bob-after-logout.png). `accountLabel()` deliberately collapses provider numeric identities to the same generic label.

**Why / fix.** [Sign-in/identity](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/90ef1565f1df46da9ade73b885db2d5bb2c4a837/docs/ui.md#kirjautuminen-ja-työtilan-valinta) makes the authenticated account, not a browser-selected Unix username, authoritative. A person must be able to check whose native work they are reading before acting, especially in shared-tab recovery. Show a safe, verified person-facing provider login/display name when available; where it is not available, say that identity cannot be displayed and keep controls safe rather than fabricate an alias. Decide the host/browser identity field with Jari before expanding the contract. This is a UX/trust finding; the observed Bob transition did **not** leak Alice's checkout.

## Polish — after defects

### 3 · Medium · The landing page repeats the entire navigation rail

**Where / experience.** At `/workspaces` the permanent rail already lists all five workspaces and their conversations, while the main area repeats the same five workspaces and links as sparse cards. The first screen is more directory than useful return-to-work surface; most of the right pane is empty. [Workspace index](screenshots/workspaces-ready.png) · [partial state](screenshots/partial-ready.png).

**Why / fix.** [The primary journey](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/90ef1565f1df46da9ade73b885db2d5bb2c4a837/docs/ui.md#käyttöliittymän-käyttökulut) is selecting a workspace/conversation and resuming reading or writing. Keep the rail as the one canonical index and make the landing pane a calm orientation with a clear recent/continue affordance derived from the current snapshot, or at minimum reduce duplicate cards. Do not build a separate recents store or speculate about activity from unverified process state. Preserve the explicit partial/empty/missing states already present.

### 4 · Low · Long native titles dominate reading space

**Where / experience.** A seeded title fills three oversized lines before any history; at 390 px it occupies much of the first content viewport below the rail. [Desktop history](screenshots/history-markdown.png) · [390px](screenshots/history-narrow.png). Content is **not** horizontally clipped (`scrollWidth = innerWidth = 390`), so this is hierarchy/density, not the old overflow defect.

**Why / fix.** The [history-reading journey](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/90ef1565f1df46da9ade73b885db2d5bb2c4a837/docs/ui.md#käyttöliittymän-käyttökulut) puts reading first and [accessibility](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/90ef1565f1df46da9ade73b885db2d5bb2c4a837/docs/ui.md#designin-lähteet-ja-toteutuksen-arviointi) requires zoom and narrow content to remain usable. Limit the title's display size/measure in the reading pane and reserve a compact but fully accessible title (expand/copy if truncated). Keep the true title available; don't silently replace it with an invented summary.

## Design questions for Jari

1. **Identity presentation versus host contract:** recommend a verified, safe account handle or display name in the rail and settings, not a Linux UID and not the indistinguishable “Oma tili”. If the existing snapshot lacks that name, decide whether a new field is justified before changing the browser/host contract. [Alice/Bob evidence](screenshots/bob-settings.png); [UI identity agreement](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/90ef1565f1df46da9ade73b885db2d5bb2c4a837/docs/ui.md#kirjautuminen-ja-työtilan-valinta).
2. **One conversation route:** the writable browser chat still uses `/workspaces/:id?conversation=pi:…`, whereas terminal history uses `/agents/:id`; visually they share a reading pane but address differently. Recommend converging on a native-ID route when host semantics are settled, not a browser-only cosmetic redirect. [Writable](screenshots/send-hylkää.png) · [native](screenshots/history-markdown.png); [agreed deep links](https://github.com/maintainer/native-agent-host/blob/90ef1565f1df46da9ade73b885db2d5bb2c4a837/docs/ui.md#kirjautuminen-ja-työtilan-valinta). This was already identified in the previous audit; no claim of a newly broken deep link.

## Previous-audit recheck and positives

- **Rejected drafts / statuses:** `hylkää` preserved the editor text, gave a specific rejection and allowed deliberate retry ([screenshot](screenshots/send-hylkää.png)); `hidas` accepted without clearing pending truth, `kaadu` showed the uncertain state and disabled unsafe resending. The initial chained script attempted `pitkä` before `hidas` had completed and timed out; that is not evidence of a product defect. A separate `pitkä` run displayed its response, but the screenshot was captured during generation rather than after completion; do not use it to certify a fully settled long answer.
- **Pagination:** nine sequential sends (Unicode + `pitkä` + seven short prompts) each displayed the matching reply, including the last turn beyond page one; [many-turn viewport](screenshots/history-many-turns.png). No 65-KiB stress claim. Unicode/3,800-character line produced no desktop overflow and the 390px conversation had `scrollWidth = innerWidth = 390` ([line](screenshots/unicode-long-line.png), [narrow](screenshots/conversation-narrow.png)).
- **Markdown:** the native assistant content uses a Markdown renderer; the initial immediate capture briefly showed the lazy renderer's raw-text fallback, which must **not** be mistaken for a permanent rendering failure. The production component renders Markdown and has a copy control; [history viewport](screenshots/history-markdown.png). Dedicated renderer tests, not this screenshot, cover inert HTML.
- **Conversation primacy / labels:** a newly selected writable conversation is the main pane, with a Finnish send state and no workspace report above the editor ([rejected conversation](screenshots/send-hylkää.png)). Old items 5 and 6 resolved. Existing native history has meaningful first-message titles in the rail, rather than four indistinguishable “Uusi keskustelu” entries.
- **First use:** sign-in is direct ([sign-in](screenshots/sign-in.png)); the chooser has a visible fixed selected count and **Jatka ilman repoja** at 390px, so the old below-the-fold action is resolved ([chooser](screenshots/chooser-narrow.png)). First selected synthetic clone returned `confirmed` before [Pi setup](screenshots/clone-selection.png). Missing, empty and partial have distinct copy ([empty](screenshots/scenario-empty.png), [missing](screenshots/scenario-missing.png), [partial](screenshots/partial-ready.png)). `delay` settled; `native-error` remained in a usable snapshot; `pi-blocked` led to the onboarding gate. These are synthetic fixture observations only.
- **Identity/keyboard:** explicit logout then Bob login in the same tab removed Alice's extra `first` workspace; `/fixture/expire` returned to [sign-in](screenshots/expired.png). A Tab on the index focused a workspace link with a computed solid 2px outline ([focus](screenshots/focused-tab.png)); this is a spot check, not a complete keyboard-only or screen-reader journey. Account menu/settings and sign-out were exercised. The previous loud always-on read-only banner is no longer present in the healthy shell ([index](screenshots/workspaces-ready.png)).
