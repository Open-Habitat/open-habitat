# Broker crash during first-login account creation

## Cause of the blocked first attempt

The first guest assertion accepted a generic broker exit as pending-journal refusal. Independent review rejected that inference. Its replacement marker at `Writer.Startup` was never observed: the restarted invocation reached `config` but not `fd`. A diagnostic guest at `9c55004` (`/var/tmp/jit-crash-retry1.err`) logged `NAH_GUEST_CONFIG_INVALID`, not `NAH_GUEST_SOCKET_UNSAFE`; `/run/native-agent-host` was root:gateway 0750. The product's `loadBrokerConfig` uses `secureFile` to read `broker.json`. That function rejects any `pending.json` **before** reading the config, before checking the socket, and before Writer startup (`internal/provision/state_linux.go`). The failure was diagnostic-fixture drift, not an observed broker availability defect: refusing to start with an uncertain account is intended.

The corrected *disposable build overlay* instruments this earlier guard with `NAH_GUEST_PENDING_REFUSED_CONFIG`; it distinguishes the later Writer marker and requires no `fd` stage in the filtered failed invocation. Neither the product source nor the security check was changed. An ordinary build, even with the inherited former guest Go tag, is scanned to exclude the hook and diagnostics. Earlier independent review and assessment of the original fixture are in `history/review-jit-broker-crash.md` and `history/assessment-jit-broker-crash.{json,md}` in the preserved worktree; neither file is committed.

## Limits and final verification

This is one account-phase SIGKILL in a disposable rootless Podman/QEMU guest, not disk-loss durability, all journal phases, recovery, unaffected sibling *browser* availability, or a bound-user test-server reboot. No Fleetrepo or host service changed, no journal was cleared and no real credentials or users were used. The issue remains open for Jari's operator-observed post-reboot smoke with bound users.

Independent final-diff review (`claude-fable-5-1`, verified by `gpt-6-astra`) identified the shared-marker ambiguity and over-specific browser-attribution wording; the distinct-marker rerun passed and the receipt now limits its claim to the sampled invocation and observed browser outcomes. The original two findings (generic exit and tagged hook) were fixed on the preserved branch. The final focused report and assessment live in gitignored `history/` scratch.
