# Synthetic lab acceptance: conversation switching (2026-10-02)

**Verdict for Jari: do not deploy this client-history lane yet.** Switching is faster and avoids repeat reads, but returning to a long controlled conversation after reading older pages jumps to the latest reply. This directly violates the requested reading-position behavior. Filed as unlaned @keep-reading-position-on-warm-return; fix and retest before the test-server release. This is a synthetic same-UID browser/host test, not a WAN or distinct-UID security measurement.

## Measurements

Production bundle on the Linux synthetic lab, fresh headless Playwright Chromium profiles, desktop 1440×900; `npm ci --prefix web`, `NAH_PORT_BASE=52930 ./scripts/lab.sh start` (actual port selected by lab: 52931), `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=$HOME/.cache/ms-playwright`. Click to visible conversation + two animation frames, local wall-clock including Playwright interaction; four SPA links among two seeded read-only conversations:

| Journey | Click-to-paint | `/api/v1/` requests during click |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Cold first history | 89 ms | 1 history GET |
| Cold second history | 86 ms | 1 history GET |
| Warm first | 82 ms | 0 |
| Warm second | 83 ms | 0 |

One EventSource connection existed across those four switches: **0 reconnects**; later reload and Alice→Bob→Alice logins opened new streams (five in the complete journey, appropriately across identity/page lifetimes). The original [baseline](measurements.md) was 108/117/100/116 ms and one history GET *on every switch* between two read-only histories. These tiny local samples demonstrate elimination of repeat requests, not a WAN p95 or guaranteed latency improvement. The committed `measure-lab.mjs` assumes a history response on every switch, so a scratch adaptation under ignored `.cache/lab/accept.mjs` waited for painted history instead. A separate long controlled-history SPA warm return measured **170 ms, zero API requests** after paging; its reading position was wrong.

## User journeys and defect

- Created a controlled fake-Pi conversation, sent a message, switched to read-only history mid-turn and back through SPA links; the intermediate text remained visible, the progress indicator stayed mounted, and the final reply arrived after releasing the fixture's intentional hold. No flicker/mask was seen in sampled states. Desktop and narrow screenshots with offline HTML twins were inspected locally. This does not prove every animation frame was flicker-free.
- Switched among the three seeded read-only histories and newly created controlled histories (>3 total), reloaded a controlled conversation and found its reply intact. An Alice draft survived reload; after logout Bob saw none of the controlled Alice message or sessionStorage keys, and Alice's draft was empty on signing back in. This is a same-UID synthetic identity test, not kernel isolation proof.
- Sent 34 fake-Pi turns in one controlled conversation. A backward `history?limit=25&before=...` GET joined older rows. In a stable sample the visible old reply moved **0.0625 px** during page insertion (within pixel rounding): the paging anchor held. Desktop and 390px captures showed the long history, with no obvious horizontal overflow. **But after clicking Home and returning via a SPA link, the visible reply changed from `pitkä historia 28` to `pitkä historia 34`**, with scrollTop 7911→16547; a second probe reproduced it. Before/after screenshots confirm the jump. The warm return generated zero API requests. @keep-reading-position-on-warm-return is release-blocking for this acceptance: the long-history reading position is not preserved. The immediate cause is not yet established; avoid treating a raw scrollTop difference alone as proof of a defect.
- A `Tapahtumia jäi väliin` banner was visible after the heavily exercised 34-turn lab session. This was not separately isolated as a product defect (the fake-Pi lab was near its child budget); inspect it during the reading-position fix/retest rather than claim journal loss on a live server.

Evidence pairs and scripts are ignored local lab artifacts at `.cache/lab/shots/snappy-*.{png,html}`, `.cache/lab/{accept,long,final-probe,midturn}.mjs` and matching `.log` files. The screenshots `snappy-scroll-before`/`snappy-scroll-after` show the specific defect; `snappy-readonly`, `snappy-controlled`, `snappy-bob`, `snappy-narrow`, `snappy-long-narrow`, `snappy-midturn` cover key states. They contain invented identities and history only. The native-history host browser test additionally has a 250-entry seeded read-only fixture, but that fixture's `pi:111…` route was **not** discoverable in lab mode, so the long lab journey used real fake-Pi sends instead. No live provider, real Pi output, real user's data, or real server was touched.

The acceptance and filed bug are documentation/issue changes only. `git diff --check`, `python3 scripts/validate-project-view.py`, and `issuectl doctor --json` were run; **the full clean gate was not rerun** because runnable code and packaged inputs did not change.

## Reading-position retest (2026-10-02)

**Release verdict: the reading-position blocker is fixed; the client-history lane may proceed to the test-server release.** The clean gate passed on committed candidate `9fef428`. The original report above describes the pre-fix build. On a fresh production-bundle synthetic lab with fake Pi, 34 controlled sends and a reload were followed by five backward history pages. The Playwright journey in `web/lab/reading-position.mjs` navigated Home and back via the sidebar twice without a reload. At the top of the older window, `pitkä historia 1` remained at 0.46875 px relative to the feed; mid-way, `pitkä historia 12` remained at -41.671875 px. Both warm returns made **zero `/api/v1/` requests**, took 260/335 ms click-to-paint (two animation frames), and showed no missed-events banner. This is synthetic same-UID local Chromium evidence, not a WAN p95 or a live-server claim. The old banner was due to the independent 64-event diagnostic status tail's normal eviction across 34 turns, not loss of the 1024-event browser journal; the UI no longer treats that status bit as a live gap when native history is present. The lab was stopped after the test.
