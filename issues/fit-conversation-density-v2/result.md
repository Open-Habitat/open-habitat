# Conversation density result

The conversation uses shared spacing tokens for a tighter phone header, transcript and composer. Supporting progress, queue and status controls scroll with the transcript, while the editor/actions remain in the viewport. The status recheck and bounded notices remain outside that scroller; the stop confirmation remains outside it so focusing the confirmation cannot steal the native reading anchor. Read-only history keeps its actions/notices in its scroller and does not invent a composer.

The [comparison pairs](../compare-v2-pairs/index.md) were recaptured from the synthetic TLS lab in light/dark at desktop and 390 × 844. Their HTML and AVIF are adjacent. No live host or personal profile was used. The fake Pi cannot produce every receipt/question outcome; the validated-response browser tests cover pending, accepted, rejected and uncertain receipts, and the synthetic lab captures cover working/answered/read-only/outage layouts. Images alone do not establish UID isolation, real Pi behavior or accessibility at every zoom factor.

The independent final-diff review was proportionate to the moderate risk: layout and scroll ownership changed, but no auth, host or contract boundary did. A focused Claude Fable review found four concrete regressions. All were corrected locally: stop focus stayed outside the stream, state/event changes restore the anchor, short viewports keep an internally scrollable composer, and notices remain visible above it rather than vanishing below the reader's position. The revalidation/reading-anchor browser test and the focused synthetic receipt/outage tests passed after the fixes. The queue now focuses its panel and restores the saved reading place on closing; the composer keeps a viewport-relative scrollable height cap for oversized content. A focused browser regression exercises queue focus/close/reopen and a 600px-tall editor on a 720px desktop viewport.
