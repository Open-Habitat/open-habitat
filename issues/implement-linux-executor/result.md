## Boundary and unknowns

This is a **bounded testable application slice**, not an activated account broker or a claim of real first-login success. The Writer leaves `pending.json` on an interrupted or failed phase and refuses silent reuse; the executor never deletes pre-existing users or automatically reconciles partial work. Root subprocesses have deadlines and bounded output, but cancellation cannot roll back an already-started OS mutation or necessarily kill descendants of site-specific hooks. The NSS `initgroups` preflight and effective systemd property spelling, ACL masks after reboot, socket ownership and real two-user routing still require synthetic-user JIT guest integration. External NSS membership changes between preflight and systemd execution remain a host policy concern. A systemd socket template may be loaded but is not started/enabled by this slice. Before pilot acceptance, `@prove-jit-guest-isolation` must observe actual NSS/home/ACL/systemd behavior, correct failures and record test-environment results; real-person OAuth, ingress and persistent Fleetrepo host declarations remain behind their separate gates.
