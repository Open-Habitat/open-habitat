# Prototype: website front page style drafts in the new brand

## Description

## Description

Jari wants style drafts of the website's front page (`website/`) in the new visual identity chosen on
13.11.2026 and specified in `brand-themes-and-state-mark`: the new mark (a rounded square ring with a
door and a resident dot), the three themes Päivä, Yö and Korkea kontrasti, and the mark's animation
language (the ring draws itself and the resident steps in through the door; the door circles the ring
while work is in progress; sub-agents orbit the resident).

This is a prototype round: several visibly different style directions of the front page, for Jari to
choose from, not a finished site. Take the colours, the mark and its CSS from that issue as they are.

Directions worth covering, each as its own draft:

- a quiet direction that follows the app closely (Päivä as default, Yö by system preference);
- a dark-first direction built on Yö's green on near-black;
- a direction where the mark and its states carry the page: the hero mark animates on load, and the
  states (thinking, working, waiting for you, done, sub-agents) explain what the product does.

Each draft shows the hero, one content section and the footer, on desktop and phone width, in day and
night, and says in one line at the top what makes it different from the others. The differences must
be visible in the default view, not behind a toggle. Text contrast at least 4.5:1; reduced motion
respected.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] At least three front-page style drafts that differ visibly, each with its one-line difference.
- [ ] Each uses the new mark, favicon and theme colours from `brand-themes-and-state-mark` unchanged.
- [ ] Desktop and phone width, day and night, for every draft.
- [ ] The hero mark's load animation and at least the working state are shown moving.
- [ ] Offered to Jari as views; his choice is recorded here before any change to the live site.
