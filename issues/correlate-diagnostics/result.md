## Decisions for review

- Follow ADR 0013 rather than the withdrawn correlation plan: do not pass OAuth attempt IDs through broker or daemon protocols and do not replace constant HTTP `correlation_id` placeholders with a support-reference system. Existing broker-verified Unix names can relate successful records; failed operations without verified identities are not forcibly joined. The Pi device attempt ID identifies only that daemon's attempt.
- Keep the shared synchronous `slog` emitter. Ignore prompt write errors, but do not claim a blocked journal writer cannot delay an operation. Bound the newly logged anonymous Access failures to a 32-record burst refilling at one per second; this only affects diagnostic emission, not admission.
- Log broker publication after its durable state transition even when subsequent daemon readiness fails; distinguish list/lookup success from route issuance. Do not attribute a human actor to root-only revocation. Suppress false daemon-failure diagnostics for browser cancellations while retaining proxy-owned timeout and live-context failure records.
